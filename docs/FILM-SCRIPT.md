## Reading tips

- Speak as if telling a friend, not reading: a little slower than you think, with a smile on the first line.
- Pause a breath at every full stop. The film adds its own pauses between lines.
- Lines 7 to 9 play over fast-forwarded footage, so keep them short and calm.
- The whole film must stay under 3:00. The preview with the placeholder voice is 2:55. If your reading is slower, say so and I will speed it up a few percent or trim a line.
